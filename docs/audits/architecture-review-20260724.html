Top recommendation · sequencing

Run the Strong tier first, then the honesty fix, then converge the engine

The formula puts the cheap, certain wins on top — delete the readiness decoy, delete the dead display loader, pin the confidence Literal. These clear noise before the substantive work. The highest-value item for this workstream is History fixtures — signed-in athletes still see fabricated VO₂max and readiness; it ranks just under the deletions only because it carries a touch more risk. The engine convergence work — session-fallback phi, tissue phi pass-through, interference double-count — is genuinely high-leverage but moves numbers feeding safety gates, so all three are gated behind a human numerical-correctness decision and must not auto-run.
